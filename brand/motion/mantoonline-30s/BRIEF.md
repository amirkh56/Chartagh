## Intent

A 30-second Instagram story/reel that showcases the Manto Online website (mantoonline.com), asks "whose work is this?", and lands on Chartagh as the answer. Chartagh must dominate the ending. Maximalist but human-made: paper, tape, torn edges, ink misregistration, handwriting, a rubber stamp. Nothing that reads as AI-generated.

## Assets

- assets/photos/p10.jpg, p23.jpg, p26.jpg, p28.jpg — Manto Online product photos (from the client's site)
- assets/mobile-hero.png, assets/desktop-hero.png — clean captures of the live site
- assets/logo-*.svg — Chartagh mark in three color variants
- assets/shamseh.svg, assets/toranj.svg — Persian ornaments from the Chartagh ornament kit

## Customizations

- Path-like journey: one continuous camera follows a hand-drawn red thread in different directions; images and text appear along it.
- Every beat holds long enough to read without pausing (≥3 s per text).
- Paper pieces move at a stop-motion cadence (12 fps, 2-frame boil) per the stop-motion-cadence law.
- Transition into the Chartagh reveal uses the torn-paper-rip block.
